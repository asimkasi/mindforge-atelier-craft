## Hourly rate — guidance

Set **$50/hr** on the profile. Reasoning: your packages are fixed-price, so the hourly
rate is mostly a positioning signal — $50 sits credibly below the $75–150/hr going rate
for Supabase/React freelancers (so you're not claiming veteran status) but far above
the $5–15 keyword-stuffed tier (so you're not signaling race-to-the-bottom quality).
Decline pure hourly engagements early on; convert interest into a package quote instead
— fixed scope is your QA safety rail. Revisit the number after 5 reviews.

Profile settings while you're in there: availability "More than 30 hrs/week" is a lie at
3–6 real hours — set **"Less than 30 hrs/week"**; leave Upwork's **AI-data-training
provisions off** for client work; turn **on** the "available now" badge only in weeks
you can actually answer within hours.
