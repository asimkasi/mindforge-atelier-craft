# Day 1 — first session, under 60 minutes

One sitting, phone + laptop at hand (ID verification wants a camera). Do it in order;
if the clock runs out, stop after step 4 — everything after is next-session work.
Have open: `upwork-profile.md` (this folder) and a photo ID.

## Setup (≈50 min)

- [ ] **(5 min) Create the Upwork account** at upwork.com → "Sign up" → work email →
      **Freelancer**. Real legal name (it must match your ID for verification later).
- [ ] **(5 min) ID verification**: complete it the moment Upwork offers it (Settings →
      Identity Verification if not prompted) — government ID photo + camera check. If
      Upwork defers it until first activity, tick this box anyway and do it at first
      prompt; never let it block a contract later.
- [ ] **(20 min) Paste the profile** from `upwork-profile.md`:
      - [ ] Title (paste as-is)
      - [ ] Overview (paste as-is; check line breaks survived)
      - [ ] Skills — all 15, in the listed order, via Upwork's autocomplete
      - [ ] Employment + education — **your real facts only**, per that file's guidance
      - [ ] Hourly rate: **$50/hr** · Availability: **"Less than 30 hrs/week"**
      - [ ] Profile photo: a real, plain photo of you (face, decent light, no logo art)
      - [ ] Portfolio: add the 3 items with the exact texts from `upwork-profile.md`
            (screenshots/PDF exports of the case-study pages as the images). If the
            app-fix case study isn't finished yet, add the other two and tick a note
            here to add it: [ ] app-fix item added later
- [ ] **(3 min) Privacy/settings sweep**: Upwork **AI-data-training provisions OFF**
      for client work · notifications → **mobile push ON** (the <24h response rule
      depends on it) · install the Upwork app on your phone now.
- [ ] **(3 min) Buy ~$15 of Connects** (≈100 Connects at ~$0.15) — Settings →
      Memberships & Connects. Stay on the free Basic plan. Log the spend in
      `VENTURE/ledger.csv` as a cost line.
- [ ] **(5 min) Set up saved searches + alerts** (from `proposal-playbook.md` §1):
      `lovable supabase` · `lovable bug` · `supabase RLS` · `lovable app fix` ·
      `bolt.new supabase` — save each, alerts on.
- [ ] **(5 min) Submit profile for review**, then log today in `VENTURE/STATE.md`:
      date, "Upwork profile submitted", Connects bought. Profile approval can take
      days — the waiting time is not idle time (see below).
- [ ] **(4 min) Skim `../clients/_packages.md` top to bottom once** — you will be asked
      "what exactly do I get?" in your first interview; the answer must be automatic.

## Not today (deliberately)

- **Fiverr gig** (`fiverr-gigs.md`) — next session, after the Upwork profile is
  approved. It's passive surface; Upwork is the channel.
- Branding, logo, website, business cards — none of it. Week 1 that is all building
  and zero conversations is the #1 red flag in the playbook.

## The weekly quota (starts the week the profile is approved)

The floor that survives a bad week — **track this, not revenue**:

- [ ] **5 tailored proposals** (via `proposal-playbook.md`: fresh <24h, <10 proposals,
      exact fit, AI-check passed — Claude drafts, you personalize and hand-submit)
      **OR 3 substantive community contributions** (only where the community permits
      AI-assisted content, you substantially rewrote and own every word, and it would
      be worth posting with zero promotional payoff)
- [ ] Total human time **≤90 min**, because Claude preps everything: start each
      session by asking Claude to shortlist matching fresh jobs and draft skeletons;
      your 90 minutes are personalization, judgment, and the submit button.
- [ ] Log in `VENTURE/STATE.md` weekly: proposals sent, replies received,
      **conversations with humans who could pay** (the week-4 diagnostic counts only
      the last number).

Budget note: at full cadence Connects run ~$40–115/month — that recurring spend is the
entire cash risk of the track. Log every top-up in the ledger; don't let it be
invisible.
