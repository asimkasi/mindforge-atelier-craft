# A better version of the prompt, for any frontier AI

The original ask — "build me AI agents that generate income, $200/month minimum,
0 restrictions" — has three problems that make any AI's answer worse:

1. **"Agents that generate income" invites fantasy.** Agents produce labor; customers
   produce income. Asking for autonomous money machines selects for the AI's most
   hallucination-prone failure mode (or for spam/scheme templates). Ask instead for a
   *business where AI does most of the labor*.
2. **"0 restrictions" is counterproductive.** It doesn't unlock anything real (no AI can
   own your payment accounts or accept a platform's ToS for you), and it invites plans
   that get your accounts banned. Constraints are what make a plan executable — give them.
3. **It states no inputs.** The strongest predictors of success — your skills, hours,
   risk tolerance, and what you already have — are missing, so the AI must guess, and it
   will guess generic.

## The improved prompt (fill in the brackets, paste anywhere)

```
I want to build a small, legitimate digital business where AI does most of the
production labor and I do the parts only a human can. Design it with me, then help
me execute it step by step.

MY INPUTS
- Skills/domains I know better than most people: [be specific — tools, industries,
  hobbies, jobs, communities you belong to]
- Time: [N] hours/week, mostly [when]. Cash I'm willing to spend: [$X/month].
- Assets I already have: [subscriptions, e.g. Claude Max; repos/projects; accounts;
  audience if any; portfolio pieces]
- Goal: $[200]/month net within [a realistic timeframe you propose — challenge me if
  my expectation is wrong], scalable beyond that.

HARD CONSTRAINTS (non-negotiable)
- Everything complies with platform terms of service. No spam, no mass cold outreach,
  no fake reviews or engagement, no plagiarism, no undisclosed AI where disclosure is
  required, no trading/crypto "income" (that's capital risk, not income).
- I personally review and approve everything before it's published, sent, or sold.
- All income is real and tax-reportable; flag anything needing professional advice.

WHAT I WANT FROM YOU
1. First, the honest base rates: for each path you propose, what fraction of people
   who try it earn $0, and why? Design around those failure modes, not past them.
2. Rank 2-3 paths by (probability of first dollar) x (speed), not by ceiling.
   Recommend ONE to start. One offer, one channel, 90 days.
3. Give me the full math: prices, platform fees, refunds, taxes — units per month
   to hit my target, and the realistic ramp week by week.
4. Tell me exactly what only I can do (accounts to open, decisions to make,
   hours to commit) and what you'll produce for my review each week.
5. Build the system so my weekly involvement is a fixed, small routine: a weekly
   review of numbers, a quota of human actions (proposals/conversations), and
   kill/scale rules we agree on in advance.
6. Push back on me. If my inputs make the goal unrealistic, say so and renegotiate
   the goal instead of flattering it.
```

## Why each change matters

- **"Business where AI does the labor" vs "agents that generate income"** — points the
  AI at operations design (its strength) instead of autonomous-money fiction (its
  failure mode).
- **Inputs section** — an AI given your unfair advantages can pick niches where you beat
  the base rate; without them it recommends what everyone gets recommended.
- **Base rates first** — forces the AI to surface the "most products sell zero" reality
  before selling you a plan, and to design for the week-5 motivation crash.
- **Probability × speed ranking** — stops ceiling-chasing ($10k/mo fantasies) and gets
  you to a first real dollar, which changes everything psychologically.
- **One offer, one channel, 90 days** — the single highest-value constraint; parallel
  launches halve everything.
- **"Push back on me"** — explicitly licenses the AI to be honest instead of agreeable.
  Frontier AIs are dramatically more useful when you make honesty the assignment.
